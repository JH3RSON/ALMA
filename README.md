## 🍃 Core Highlights & Philosophy

ALMA is designed to be intentional, uncluttered, and deeply personal:

- 🛡️ **Absolute Offline Privacy:** Your entries, memories, and photos never touch an external cloud or analytics server. Everything stays securely on your local device.
- 🧘 **Designed for Calm:** A distraction-free, fluid interface sculpted to encourage mindful writing and self-reflection without noise or clutter.
- 📖 **Daily Philosophical Reflections:** Receive inspiring daily quotes and curated thoughts to prompt deep thinking, perspective, and personal growth.
- 🖼️ **Visual Journaling:** Effortlessly enrich your entries by attaching meaningful pictures and moments alongside your written words.
- ⚡ **Pure Native Kotlin Performance:** Built entirely from scratch in 100% modern Kotlin for instant responsiveness, buttery-smooth scrolling, and zero lag.
- 🆓 **100% Open-Source & Non-Profit:** No trackers, no data harvesting, no paywalls, and no subscription traps.
